# STELLARIS Astronomy Reference Library — Catalogue

Generated by `tools/write_catalog.py` from `metadata/manifest.json` (per-file SHA-256, URL, credit).
Files are downloaded by `tools/acquire_assets.py` and are not committed (size); the manifest is.
Kind: unless stated, entries are processed observatory images. Titles that say *artist's impression*,
*illustration* or *simulation* are not observations; they are excluded from the Observation view.
Type Ia remnants (Tycho, Kepler) are reference only: STELLARIS follows single stars, and a Type Ia
supernova needs a white dwarf in a binary.
